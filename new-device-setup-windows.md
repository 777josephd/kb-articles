# Setting Up a New Work Device (Windows)

**Audience:** All staff receiving a new or replacement Windows laptop or desktop  
**Last updated:** 2026  
**Maintained by:** IT Help Desk

---

## Overview

Getting a new work device is exciting — but there are a few important steps to complete before you start using it for work. This guide walks you through what to expect and what you need to do on your end. IT will handle the technical configuration, but some steps require your involvement.

If your device was set up and delivered by IT, some of these steps may already be complete. Start from the beginning and skip any steps that are already done.

---

## Step 1 — Sign In with Your Work Account

When you power on the device for the first time, you will be prompted to sign in. Use your standard work email address and password — the same credentials you use for email and other work applications.

If you are prompted to set up Windows Hello (fingerprint or face recognition), you can complete this now or skip it and set it up later. It is optional but convenient.

---

## Step 2 — Complete MFA Enrollment

After signing in, you may be prompted to set up multi-factor authentication if it has not been configured on this device yet. Follow the on-screen steps and use your authenticator app to complete enrollment.

If you run into issues during MFA setup, refer to the **MFA Troubleshooting Guide** in this KB or contact the Help Desk before proceeding.

---

## Step 3 — Connect to the Network

**In the office:** Connect to the corporate Wi-Fi network using your work credentials when prompted, or plug in to a wired network connection at your desk.

**Working remotely:** Connect to your home or external network first, then open the VPN client and connect before accessing any internal resources. Refer to the **VPN Connectivity FAQ** in this KB if you need help.

---

## Step 4 — Allow Windows Updates to Complete

New devices often have pending software updates that need to install before the device is fully ready. Go to **Settings → Windows Update → Check for Updates** and allow any available updates to install. This may require a restart.

Do not skip this step. Updates patch security vulnerabilities and ensure the device is compliant with organizational policy.

---

## Step 5 — Verify Your Applications Are Installed

Check that the applications you need for your role are installed and working. Common applications include:

- Microsoft Outlook (email)
- Microsoft Teams
- VPN client
- Any role-specific software your manager identified during onboarding

If an application you need is missing, contact the Help Desk. Do not download software from the internet without IT approval — all software installations should go through IT to ensure compatibility and security compliance.

---

## Step 6 — Map Your Shared Drives

If your role requires access to shared network drives, these may need to be connected manually on a new device. Your manager or IT contact can provide the drive path. To connect a shared drive:

1. Open **File Explorer**
2. Click **This PC** in the left panel
3. Click **Map network drive** at the top
4. Enter the drive path provided by IT and check **Reconnect at sign-in**

If you receive an access error when connecting, contact the Help Desk — you may need permissions added to your account.

---

## Step 7 — Transfer Files From Your Old Device (If Applicable)

If you are replacing an existing device, do not transfer files by copying them to a USB drive unless IT has approved this method. The preferred approach is:

- Files stored on shared drives or SharePoint are already accessible from your new device — no transfer needed
- Personal files saved locally on your old device should be reviewed with IT before moving

Contact the Help Desk if you need assistance retrieving files from a previous device.

---

## Common Issues During Setup

**"Your account doesn't have permission to complete setup"**
Your account may not yet be fully provisioned. Contact the Help Desk with your username and the device name (found on a sticker on the bottom of the laptop).

**Device asks for a BitLocker recovery key**
This can happen after certain updates or hardware changes. Contact the Help Desk immediately — do not attempt to guess the key. We can retrieve it for you.

**Outlook is not receiving email or prompts for a password repeatedly**
Sign out of Outlook completely, then sign back in with your full work email address. If the issue continues, contact the Help Desk.

---

## When to Contact the Help Desk

- Any step above is not working as described
- An application you need is missing and not available through the company software portal
- You are unable to access shared drives after following the steps above
- Your device is prompting for a BitLocker recovery key

---

## What to have ready when you contact us

- Your **full name** and **employee ID**
- The **device name or asset tag** (on a sticker on the bottom of the device)
- The **specific step** where you encountered an issue
- Any **error message** you are seeing
