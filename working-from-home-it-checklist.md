# Working From Home — IT Checklist

**Audience:** All staff working remotely  
**Last updated:** 2026  
**Maintained by:** IT Help Desk

---

## Overview

Working from home introduces a few IT considerations that do not apply when you are in the office. This guide covers what you need to have set up, what to check when something is not working, and how to get help quickly if you need it.

Think of this as your remote work IT reference — bookmark it and come back to it whenever something stops working.

---

## Before You Start Working Remotely

Make sure these are in place before your first remote session. Setting them up in advance prevents the most common remote work issues.

### ✅ VPN client is installed and you know how to connect
Your VPN client should be installed on your work device before you leave the office. If it is not installed, contact the Help Desk while you still have access to the office network — installing it remotely is more complicated.

Test your VPN connection at least once before you need to rely on it.

### ✅ MFA is set up on your phone
You will need MFA to log in remotely. Make sure your authenticator app is installed and working. Refer to the **MFA Troubleshooting Guide** in this KB if you need help setting it up.

### ✅ You know where your files are
Files saved only to your local desktop or Downloads folder are not accessible from other devices and are not backed up. Make sure your important files are stored on a shared drive or in SharePoint so you can access them from anywhere.

### ✅ You have the Help Desk contact information saved
If something stops working while you are remote, you will need a way to reach us that does not depend on the thing that is broken. Save the Help Desk phone number and email somewhere outside your work applications — in your phone, for example.

## Daily Connection Checklist

Run through this at the start of each remote work session if you are experiencing issues:

- [ ] Is your home internet working? (Test by opening a website without VPN)
- [ ] Are you connected to VPN? (Check the VPN client — it should show as connected)
- [ ] Are your shared drives accessible? (Open File Explorer and check under This PC)
- [ ] Is your email loading? (Open Outlook or your mail app)
- [ ] Is Teams or your communication platform connected?

If any of these are not working, refer to the relevant KB article or contact the Help Desk.

## The Most Common Remote Work Issues and What to Check First

### "I can't connect to VPN"
1. Check that your home internet is working without VPN
2. Restart the VPN client
3. Restart your computer
4. Check that your computer's clock is set to update automatically — an incorrect clock can prevent VPN authentication

See the **VPN Connectivity FAQ** for full troubleshooting steps.

---

### "VPN is connected but I can't access my files or internal sites"
1. Disconnect from VPN, wait 30 seconds, reconnect
2. If shared drives are missing, try remapping them (see the **Shared Drive Access Issues** guide)
3. If an internal website is not loading, try restarting the VPN client entirely

---

### "My MFA codes aren't working"
1. Check that your phone's clock is set to update automatically
2. Close and reopen your authenticator app
3. Generate a fresh code and enter it immediately — codes expire after 30 seconds

See the **MFA Troubleshooting Guide** for full troubleshooting steps.

---

### "Everything is very slow"
1. Test your internet speed at fast.com without VPN — if it is slow without VPN, your home network is the issue
2. Restart your router (unplug it, wait 30 seconds, plug it back in)
3. Close any applications you are not using — video calls and large file syncs use significant bandwidth
4. If possible, connect your laptop to your router with an ethernet cable rather than Wi-Fi

---

### "I can't access something that worked fine in the office"
Some resources — certain internal websites, printers, or applications — may only be accessible on the office network even when you are on VPN, depending on how they are configured. If you cannot access a specific resource remotely and VPN is connected, contact the Help Desk. We can confirm whether remote access to that resource is supported.

## Security Reminders for Remote Work

These are not optional — they protect both you and the organization.

- **Only use your work device for work tasks** when accessing organizational systems. Personal devices introduce security risks that the organization cannot manage.
- **Do not work from public Wi-Fi without VPN connected.** Coffee shops, airports, and hotels are not secure networks.
- **Lock your screen** when you step away, even at home. Use Windows+L to lock instantly.
- **Do not share your screen** during video calls unless you have closed any windows containing sensitive information.
- **Report anything suspicious** — unexpected MFA prompts, strange emails, or applications behaving oddly. Contact the Help Desk if something does not feel right.

---

## When to Contact the Help Desk

- You cannot connect to VPN and the troubleshooting steps above did not help
- You are locked out of your account
- You cannot access a resource you need for your work and VPN is connected
- Something on your device is behaving unexpectedly
- You received a suspicious email or MFA prompt you did not initiate

---

## What to have ready when you contact us

- Your **full name** and **employee ID or username**
- The **device you are using** (work laptop model, operating system)
- Your **current location** — in the office, at home, or another location
- Whether you are on **Wi-Fi or wired**
- The **specific issue** you are experiencing and what you have already tried
- Any **error messages** you are seeing
