# How to Connect to a Printer and Fix Common Printing Issues

**Audience:** All staff  
**Last updated:** 2026  
**Maintained by:** IT Help Desk

---

## Overview

Printing issues are one of the most common help desk requests. Most of them fall into two categories: your computer cannot find the printer, or the printer itself has stopped working. This guide helps you identify which situation you are in and what to do about it.

---

## Connecting to a Printer for the First Time

If you have a new device or you have never connected to an office printer before, follow these steps:

1. Click the **Start menu** and open **Settings**
2. Go to **Bluetooth & devices → Printers & scanners**
3. Click **Add a printer or scanner** and wait for your computer to search
4. Select the correct printer from the list and click **Add device**

If the printer does not appear in the list, it may need to be added manually using its network address. Contact the Help Desk and we will provide the correct printer path and walk you through adding it.

**Note:** You must be connected to the office network or VPN for network printers to appear.

## Common Printing Issues and What to Try

### My print job is not printing — it just disappears or gets stuck

This usually means the job is stuck in the print queue, or the printer's background service on your computer has stopped responding.

**Try this:**

1. Open the **Start menu**, search for **Printers & scanners**, and open it
2. Click the printer you are trying to use and select **Open print queue**
3. If there are jobs listed, right-click each one and select **Cancel**
4. Once the queue is clear, try printing again

If jobs keep getting stuck, restart your computer and try again. If the issue continues, contact the Help Desk — the printer's background service may need to be reset on your machine.

---

### The printer I always use has disappeared from my list

This can happen after a Windows update or if your computer's connection to the network printer was dropped.

**Try this:**

1. Make sure you are connected to the office network or VPN
2. Go to **Settings → Printers & scanners** and click **Add a printer or scanner**
3. Wait for the search to complete and see if your printer reappears
4. If it does, add it again — your settings and preferences should carry over

If the printer does not reappear after searching, contact the Help Desk. The printer itself or the print server may need attention.

---

### The printer is showing as offline even though it is powered on

"Offline" in Windows does not always mean the printer is actually off — it sometimes means Windows lost track of it.

**Try this:**

1. Go to **Settings → Printers & scanners** and click the printer
2. Click **Open print queue**
3. In the menu bar of the print queue window, click **Printer** and look for **Use Printer Offline** — if it has a checkmark, click it to uncheck it
4. Try printing again

If the printer is still showing as offline, try turning the printer off, waiting 30 seconds, and turning it back on. If it is a shared office printer and multiple people are affected, contact the Help Desk — the issue is likely on the printer or server side, not your computer.

---

### Everyone in the office has stopped being able to print

If multiple people are affected at the same time, this is not an issue with individual computers — it is most likely a problem with the print server or the printer itself.

**Do not try to fix this yourself.** Contact the Help Desk and let us know:
- Which printer is affected
- Roughly how many people are impacted
- When it last worked

We will address it from the server side.

---

## When to Contact the Help Desk

- You cannot find the printer you need and adding it manually did not work
- Print jobs keep getting stuck or disappearing and restarting did not help
- The printer shows as offline and the steps above did not resolve it
- Multiple people are unable to print to the same printer
- You need access to a printer in a different area or floor of the building

---

## What to have ready when you contact us

- Your **full name** and **employee ID**
- The **name or location of the printer** you are trying to use (usually printed on a label on the device)
- The **error message** you are seeing, if any
- Whether this is happening **only on your computer** or for **multiple people**
- Whether you are working **in the office or remotely**
- When it **last worked**
