# Account Lockout — What to Do

**Audience:** All staff  
**Last updated:** 2026  
**Maintained by:** IT Help Desk

---

## What is an account lockout?

An account lockout happens when too many incorrect password attempts are detected on your account in a short period of time. As a security measure, the system automatically locks the account to prevent unauthorized access. This can happen even if you are the one entering the password — a few mistyped attempts is enough to trigger it.

Lockouts are common and are not a sign that your account has been hacked. They are usually resolved quickly.

---

## Common Reasons Your Account Gets Locked

Before calling the Help Desk, it helps to know why this happens — because the same cause will lock you out again if it is not addressed.

**Saved passwords that are out of date**
This is the most common cause. If you recently changed your password, your phone, tablet, or another device may still be trying to connect using the old one. Every failed attempt counts against you, even from a device you forgot about.

**Mapped drives or applications running in the background**
Work applications — email clients, shared drives, VPN clients — often try to reconnect automatically. If they have an old password saved, they will keep trying and lock your account repeatedly, even after the Help Desk unlocks it.

**Someone else attempting to access your account**
Less common, but possible. If your account locks repeatedly with no explanation, let the Help Desk know — this is worth investigating.

---

## What to Do When You Are Locked Out

1. **Stop attempting to log in.** Additional failed attempts will not help and may extend the lockout window.
2. **Contact the Help Desk.** We can unlock your account after verifying your identity. Have your employee ID or username ready.
3. **Tell us which devices you use to access work.** This helps us identify what triggered the lockout.
4. **Update your saved password on all devices after your account is unlocked.** This is the step most people skip — and why they get locked out again the same day.

---

## Updating Your Saved Password After a Lockout

Once your account is unlocked and you have successfully logged in with your new or current password, update it everywhere it is saved:

- **Your phone** — go to Settings → Passwords (iPhone) or Settings → Passwords & Accounts (Android) and update any saved work credentials
- **Your email app** — if you use Outlook or another mail app on your phone, it will prompt you to re-enter your password
- **Your VPN client** — if your VPN saves credentials, update them before connecting
- **Any browser that saved your password** — Chrome, Edge, and Safari all store passwords; update them in the browser's password manager

If your account locks again within a few hours of being unlocked, one of these sources is still using the old password. Call us back and we will help identify which device is causing it.

---

## When to Contact the Help Desk

- Your account is locked and you cannot log in
- Your account has been unlocked but keeps locking again
- You did not change your password recently but are being prompted to log in again
- You believe someone else may be attempting to access your account

---

## What to have ready when you contact us

- Your **full name** and **employee ID or username**
- The **device and application** you were using when the lockout occurred
- A list of **other devices** you use to access work (phone, home laptop, tablet, etc.)
- Approximately **when the lockout started**
